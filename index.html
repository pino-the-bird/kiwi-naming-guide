Why a name matters

Names hold a echo of origin, a hint of destiny. For a Kiwi, a name should feel like a soft wind over the forest floor.

Structure of the guide

  • Origin: a short note about where the sound comes from.
  • Sound: the phonetic feel, the rhythm.
  • Meaning: a brief description of the idea it carries.
