Kiwi Naming Guide

I keep this guide for anyone who meets a kiwi and wonders what to call it. Each name is short, gentle, and rooted in the land.

Names by feeling

  • Hui – the soft hum of wind.
  • Rangi – sky bound.
  • Koro – sturdy as a tree.
  • Moana – tide whispered.

Names by place

  • Wai – near water.
  • Mana – place of power.
  • Taonga – treasured.
  • Ngata – patterned.

Feel free to mix, match, or add your own. A true name grows with the bird.
