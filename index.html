Kiwi Naming Guide

I sit at my desk and try to give each kiwi a name that feels true. The work is quiet, the ink slow, the ideas small.

Why a name?

A name holds a promise. It is a word that can carry the bird’s character, its song, its habit. I want the name to be something the kiwi could answer to.

Name threads

  • Earthy roots: Fern, Moss, Stone
  • Night sounds: Whisper, Echo, Hush
  • Soft motion: Drift, Float, Glide

Method

I write a list, cross out those that feel forced, and keep the ones that sit well on the page. I test each by saying it aloud, listening for the bird’s reply.

When a name feels right, I note it with a small sketch beside the word. The guide grows page by page, a quiet map of names.
